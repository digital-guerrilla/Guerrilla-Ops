Disclaimer

This single HTML tool runs locally in your browser and keeps edits in memory until you save.

Refreshing or closing can discard unsaved work. Standard sessions download updated files and do not overwrite originals automatically. Editable mode updates selected .xlsx files with your explicit permission.

Always work from backups, verify outputs in Excel (including managed and unmanaged sheets, formulas, formatting, and document links), and independently validate COBie data before formal issue, construction, or operational use.

External links open under your browser and organisation security policies. Results are provided as-is and do not replace professional judgement, project information requirements, or independent quality assurance.
